Mergers & Acquisitions

An interactive course on the Sale and Purchase Agreement and the full M&A deal lifecycle. Jurisdictional focus: United Kingdom, United States (Delaware), European Union.

This course is built for a non-lawyer working at a law firm — to give you the depth needed to sit in a room with M&A lawyers and follow what they're saying, ask the right questions, and understand the commercial stakes behind every clause.

Each module pairs concept explanations with jurisdictional callouts (where UK / US / EU practice diverges), examples lifted from a real Italian-law SPA training deck, and negotiation context (what each side typically pushes for). Use the practice modes to lock in retention.

Practice modes

Active recall locks in what you've read. Three modes, each spaced for different purposes:

  • Flashcards — term → definition recall with self-grade spaced repetition. Best for terminology.
  • Multiple-choice quizzes — concept questions with carefully-built distractors (e.g., tipping basket vs. deductible basket). Best for sharpening discrimination between similar concepts.
  • Clause-spotting / scenarios — short clause excerpts and fact patterns asking "What's wrong with this from buyer's perspective?" Closest to actual M&A practice.

Reference

Glossary — every defined term with full name (no abbreviations), short definition, extended explanation, jurisdictional notes, and cross-references. Searchable.

How to use this course

  • Order: The modules are designed to be read in numerical order. Modules 00–06 set up the foundations; 07–14 are the SPA-specific deep dive (Session 7 of the source deck); 15–18 cover ancillary documents, dispute resolution, post-closing, and public M&A.
  • Pace: Each module is dense. Don't try to do more than two or three modules in a single sitting.
  • Recall: After every two modules, do 10 minutes of flashcards filtered to those modules. Do scenarios after every four modules.
  • Progress: The course remembers what you've read in your browser's local storage.
Source material: Session 7 — Introduction to SPA and legal documents in M&A transactions, Accuracy / Ughi e Nunziante / ESCP Business School, Academic Year 2025–2026. This course extends well beyond the source deck.
